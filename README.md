# Sundance

An isometric, pixel-art spaghetti western in Godot 4, drawing on Leone, Corbucci and Sollima.
One stranger, the border town of San Miguel, ten bandits. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

## Play

Open the folder in Godot 4.7 and press Play, or run:

```bash
godot --path .
```

WASD move · mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB fan the Colt / fire both barrels · C or Ctrl crouch ·
R reload (fire stops a partial reload) · 1/2/3 or wheel to switch guns · E knife (hold to drag
a body; at the gallows, hold to cut the condemned man loose) · B bandage · G throw dynamite · F swap for the gun at your feet · Enter restart · Esc quit.

On the loadout screen: 1/2/3 or a click go through a slot's guns (those you can afford), B and G
buy a bandage and a stick of dynamite, Enter rides in. Each gun and piece of kit costs points out
of the level's budget.

## Assets

Every sprite and sound is generated by `tools/gen_assets.py`: a small point-splat 3D renderer
draws the 8-direction characters, buildings and props in 2:1 isometric pixel art, and the
sounds are synthesised. Each map is laid out by its own script, `tools/make_town.py`
(San Miguel, `data/town.json`) and `tools/make_cruz_verde.py` (`data/cruz_verde.json`),
sharing `tools/layout.py`, which refuses overlapping objects or anyone placed inside
things and writes the guard posts. A map with a `"ground"` key gets its own ground
baked by `gen_assets.py ground`. Character animations are baked in parallel from key poses in
`tools/chars.py` and packed into one atlas per character (about a minute on a multi-core machine). The town layout lives in `data/town.json`.

```bash
python3 -m venv tools/.venv && tools/.venv/bin/pip install -r tools/requirements.txt
tools/.venv/bin/python tools/gen_assets.py            # everything
tools/.venv/bin/python tools/gen_assets.py chars ui   # or just some: chars world fx ui sfx animals ground
SUNDANCE_TEMPLATES=veteran tools/.venv/bin/python tools/gen_assets.py chars   # re-bake some bodies
SUNDANCE_OBJECTS=coop,hencrate tools/.venv/bin/python tools/gen_assets.py world   # re-bake some world objects
godot --headless --path . --import
```

Flags go after `--`: `--skip-title` (no title or loadout screen; rides in with Colt, coach gun and Winchester, or `--guns=navy,spencer`, one a slot), `--level=ID` (a level from `data/levels.json`, e.g. `san_miguel_35`), `--at=GX,GY` (start tile), `--bot` (autopilot that shoots
and logs hits and bandit reactions; `--bot=miss` shoots just past them), `--alert` (trigger the close-up), `--rattle` (near-miss every bandit once), `--freeze` (no title, bandits stand still), `--shot=PATH --shot-time=SECONDS` (save a
screenshot and quit).
